# Brand assets: a0-plugin-jev-compact

PROPOSAL: the owner signs off logos. Nothing here is final until they do.

Part of the Patchbay family (see `DESIGN.md`). Hand-written SVG, generated by
`family-build.py`; no raster sources, no stock icons, no generated imagery.

## Family construction

- 64 x 64 grid.
- Frame: rounded square, outer edge 4..60, outer corner radius 8, 6-unit stroke, no fill.
- Pin: 14 x 14 block in the signal colour in the bottom-right corner, continuing the frame outline.
- Glyph box: 28 x 28 at 16..44, 4-unit strokes, butt caps, mitre joins, straight segments only.

## This plugin's glyph

Two chevrons squeezing a kept bar. The chevrons close in from top and bottom
(compaction); the bar between them is the span that survives verbatim. Path:
`M20 19L30 25L40 19 M19 30H41 M20 41L30 35L40 41`.

## Rebuild

```sh
python3 assets/brand/family-build.py jev-compact assets/brand
resvg assets/brand/wordmark.svg assets/brand/wordmark.png   # likewise for the other SVGs
oxipng -o 4 assets/brand/*.png
```
